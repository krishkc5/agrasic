AgriASIC digital die

planned architecture · tetrapolar analog front end

Analog front end is tetrapolar: the sine DAC and drive buffer excite E1, the high-impedance differential PGA reads E2 − E3, the TIA converts the return current at E4, and two phase-locked sample-and-holds feed one differential SAR ADC through the mux, all about VCM = 0.9 V. The digital side computes Z(f) = ΔV(f) / I(f) from the two channels. Open decision: simultaneous S/H on both channels (preserves V/I phase directly, two matched switches) versus alternating cycles phase-locked to the same counter (one S/H, longer measurement). The drawing shows the simultaneous option.
